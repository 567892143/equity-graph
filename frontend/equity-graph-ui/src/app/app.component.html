Home Connection Finder

EquityGraph is a corporate network intelligence platform designed to uncover hidden systemic relationship risks between enterprises across director interlocks, multi-tier supply chains, and institutional ownership stakes.

Relationship Graph Types

Board Interlock

The same director or executive serves on the boards of both companies, creating governance cross-influence.

Supply Chain Exposure

One company depends on another as a key vendor or supplier, measuring operational disruption exposure.

Institutional Overlap

The same institutional investor or asset manager holds significant equity stakes across both companies.
